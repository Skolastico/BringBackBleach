Subtle changes. Longer review pending.

Daily image journal / 31 October 2026 / Nightly dispatch · 31 October 2026

The new test keeps the drawing close to the previous version. Fine-detail measurements improved in short scenes from episodes 5 and 8, but visible differences remain subtle. The test stays experimental; episode-length validation is pending.

The original DVD picture can look soft. Restoring texture is useful only if faces, outlines and motion still look right. A score can improve even when a person cannot see a worthwhile change.

The previous version remains our reference. The new test showed no obvious new distortion in reviewed stills, but has not been adopted.

The image, before and after

Before · previous version
Previous version: the reference used before today’s test. This is a crop of Orihime in episode 8, about seven minutes into the episode.
After · newer test
New test: the identical moment and crop. Look at the cheek, eye and hair outlines. They remain very similar; better measurements do not establish a visible improvement.

Both pictures come from the same frame. This is a still-image review; it cannot tell us whether the result looks stable in motion.
