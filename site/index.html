Daily image journal / 30 October 2026 / DVD07 · review complete

A selective test lowered the fine-detail error score, but reviewers could not see a clear gain in the action frame. Its structural result missed the preset target, so DVD07 was not promoted.

The project is trying to recover useful image detail without changing the larger drawing. In this round, the fine-detail measure moved, but the structural gain was too small to pass the agreed review gate.

DVD05 remains the strongest candidate. The next step is to find new, well-matched scenes before considering another limited experiment.

The image, before and after

Before · DVD05 candidate
DVD05 entered the review as the strongest candidate. In the same EP007 action-and-effects crop, fine-detail error was 0.0008194. The enlarged frame already looked close to the comparison target.
After · DVD07 test
DVD07 lowered the score to 0.0006923, a 15.52% change. The enlarged review still showed no clear visible gain; the masked structure result improved just 0.82%, below the 5% gate.

This is a static action-and-effects validation crop already seen during development. Fine-detail error measures a small-scale mismatch against a historical proxy target; it is not a viewer score or proof of authentic film grain.

The image pair is a small, same-frame crop from the reviewed EP007 action-and-effects validation scene. The gallery below contains separate EP008 archive examples from an earlier v7 test.

The open questions behind the pictures.

Fine detail can improve on paper without looking different.
Keep the visual review alongside the measurement. DVD07 stays experimental because its action sample showed no clear visible gain.
Small structural changes are not enough to justify a new candidate.
The structural gate was set at 5%. DVD07 reached 0.82%, so the existing DVD05 candidate remains the reference.
There are too few well-matched action and effects scenes.
Look for new paired scenes in EP001–EP006; keep EP007 out of new training and reserved for validation.
A still image cannot confirm stable motion.
Review timing and frame-to-frame consistency before making any claim about a full episode or flicker.
